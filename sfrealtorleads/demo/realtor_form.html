Contact Form

This is a demo form when you can try the automatic respond of SF Realtor Leads App

How Does it work?
First, enter an email address you have access to in the "real estate agent's email" field. You will receive a copy of the message that is sent to the real estate agent whenever a user visits their website and fills out the contact form. This field is included solely so that you can see the email the agent will receive in their personal inbox; it is not part of the system itself.

Client Details

Then, fill out the client's fields. This fields pretend to be the realtor website's contact form. You can enter a different email address from the realtor email address in "email" field so that you can see how it works in both email address (realtor email and client email). However, you can enter the same email address in both fields if you don't have access to another email account. Both emails going to be sent at the same email address, one for realtor and one for client.
